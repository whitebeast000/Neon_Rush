Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a free 2D endless runner that plays in your browser. Run through a glowing neon city, jump over blocks and pit gaps, slide under overhead barriers and collect coins. The runner moves automatically and gets faster the longer you survive, so timing matters more than speed.

Controls

On a computer, press Space or the Up arrow to jump, the Down arrow to slide and Esc to pause. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen Jump and Slide buttons. Landscape orientation works best.
