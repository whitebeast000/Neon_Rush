Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Neon Rush (the "Site", at https://neon-rush-plum.vercel.app) handles information when you visit the Site and play the game.

Information we store

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through the game. Your best score, coin total and settings are saved in your own browser using local storage. This data stays on your device and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, visitors will be asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Log data and hosting

The Site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operation. Google may also collect similar technical data when ads are loaded.

Links to other sites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
