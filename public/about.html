About Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a free 2D endless runner that plays in your browser. There is nothing to download, no account to create and nothing to pay. You guide a runner through a glowing futuristic city, jumping over blocks and pit gaps, sliding under overhead barriers and collecting coins for as long as you can survive.

The idea behind the game

Endless runners are some of the easiest games to understand and some of the hardest to put down. We wanted to make one that teaches itself in a few seconds but keeps rewarding practice. The runner moves forward on its own, so the only decisions you make are about timing: when to jump, when to slide and which coins are worth chasing. That small set of choices is enough to create tense, fast runs where one mistake ends everything.

How it is built

Neon Rush is an independent project made with JavaScript, React, Vite and the Phaser 3 game engine. Phaser draws and runs the game world, while React handles the menus, the heads-up display, the pause screen and the results screen. The two parts talk to each other through a small event system. All sound effects are generated directly in your browser, so the game has no audio files to load. There is no server and no database: your best score, total coins and settings are stored in your own browser using local storage.

What you can do in the game

Chase a higher best score, collect coins across many runs, and adjust the sound effects and the reduced-effects option in Settings if your device is slower. Controls are simple on every device. On a computer, press Space or the Up arrow to jump, the Down arrow to slide and Esc to pause. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen Jump and Slide buttons. The game plays best with the phone held in landscape orientation.

Guides and articles

Alongside the game we publish written guides for players who want to improve: tips for a higher score, an explanation of how the speed and difficulty system works, a broader look at endless runner strategy, and a development log about how the game was made and what we plan next.
