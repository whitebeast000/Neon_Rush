About Neon Rush

Neon Rush is a free 2D endless runner game made for the browser. It was designed and built as an independent project using React and the Phaser game engine. It runs on computers, tablets and phones with no download, no account and no payment.

The idea

We wanted a game that is easy to understand in seconds but hard to master. You run through a neon city, jump over obstacles, slide under barriers and collect coins. The speed increases over time, so every run is a little different.

What you can do

Chase your best score, collect coins across runs and tune the sound and visual effects in Settings. If your device is slower, turn on Reduced effects for smoother play. Your progress is stored only in your own browser.

Keep in touch

We are always improving the game. If you have feedback, a bug report or an idea, please visit our contact page.
