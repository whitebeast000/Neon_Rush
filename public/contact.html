When reporting a bug

Please tell us which device and browser you use, whether you were in portrait or landscape, and what happened just before the problem. A short description of how to repeat it helps us find and fix it quickly. We usually reply within a few days.

Feature ideas

Moving obstacles, background music and a portrait-friendly layout are on our wish list. If you have a different idea, tell us. Player suggestions directly shape what we build next.
